# Integrity & Consistency Risk Report

## Executive Summary
The manuscript demonstrates high internal consistency in its numerical claims, experimental setup, and arithmetic across tables and text. The primary integrity/consistency concern is a **clear factual mismatch in layer indexing** between the main results table, the textual description, and the visualization figure. This off-by-one discrepancy affects reproducibility and clarity. All other claims, metrics, and methodological details align coherently. The paper transparently discloses automated generation, and future-dated references (2025) are internally consistent with this framing.

## 1. Critical Inconsistencies & Logical Issues

### 🔴 Layer Indexing Mismatch (Table 1 vs. Figure 3 vs. Text)
- **Claim/Anchor:** Table 1 lists the NLL-Guided selected layers as `[1, 3, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15, 21, 34]`. Section 4.5 explicitly states: *"Layer 15 shows the highest $\Delta$-NLL (0.011)..."*
- **Contradiction:** Figure 3 (`Per-Layer NLL Degradation...`) uses a 0-indexed x-axis (0–35). The blue bars (selected layers) appear at indices `1, 3, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 21, 34`. The highest bar is at index **14**, not 15. Index 15 in the figure is gray (not selected).
- **Impact:** This indicates a 0-based vs. 1-based indexing confusion between the table/text and the figure. While likely a minor plotting or labeling error, it creates a direct factual mismatch that could mislead implementation or reproducibility efforts.
- **Recommendation:** Standardize indexing across all tables, figures, and text (explicitly state whether layers are 0-indexed or 1-indexed) and correct Figure 3 or Table 1 to align.
