# Integrity and Consistency Risk Report

**Manuscript Title:** ENTROPY DYNAMICS DO NOT PROVIDE RELIABLE EXECUTION-FREE SELECTION SIGNALS FOR CODE GENERATION

## 1. Critical Internal Inconsistencies

### 1.1. Contradiction Between Figure 1 Diagram and Text/Caption Regarding Selection Direction
There is a direct contradiction between the visual diagram in Figure 1 and the textual description of the **nEDIS_v2** method.

*   **Text (Section 3.3):** Explicitly states that nEDIS_v2 uses **"argmax selection (higher score = selected)"** and that this modification "inverts the selection direction" from the original argmin.
*   **Figure 1 Caption:** States the pipeline selects the candidate with the **"highest nEDIS_v2 score."**
*   **Figure 1 Diagram:** The "Best-of-N Selection" box explicitly displays **"argmin nEDIS"** and includes the label **"Lower = More Reliable."** Furthermore, the diagram shows the formula `nEDIS = s(y) * (1 + CV_H^2)` without the length multiplication (`* T`) defined in Equation 4 for nEDIS_v2.

**Impact:** The diagram illustrates the *original* nEDIS method (argmin), while the caption and text describe the *optimized* nEDIS_v2 method (argmax). This misrepresents the final method evaluated in the experiments.

### 1.2. Validity of "Pre-Registered" Claims in an Automated Context
The manuscript claims rigorous experimental design via pre-registration, but this conflicts with the disclosure of automated generation.

*   **Text (Abstract/Section 3.4):** Claims the study uses **"pre-registered success criteria"** and a **"pre-registered refutation condition"** to ensure rigor and avoid p-hacking.
*   **Footnote 1:** States: **"WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system."**

**Impact:** In scientific practice, pre-registration implies an independent, time-stamped commitment made *before* data analysis to prevent bias. An automated system generating a paper typically produces the text *after* running experiments. If the "pre-registration" is merely a narrative component generated by the system post-hoc (or internal logic), it does not provide the external safeguard against bias that the term implies. This undermines the claim of rigorous, bias-free experimental design.

## 3. Conclusion

No major numerical mismatches or logical contradictions were found in the results reporting or statistical analysis. The primary integrity risk stems from the **misleading Figure 1**, which depicts the wrong selection logic (argmin instead of argmax) for the method being evaluated. Additionally, the claim of **pre-registration** is questionable given the automated nature of the paper's generation, potentially overstating the rigor of the experimental design.
