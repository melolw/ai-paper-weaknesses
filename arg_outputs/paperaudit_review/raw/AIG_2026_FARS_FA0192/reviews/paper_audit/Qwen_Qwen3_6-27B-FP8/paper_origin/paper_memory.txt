# Global Summary
This paper investigates whether autoregressive-order (AR-order) reinforcement learning post-training compromises the inherent "order robustness" of diffusion language models (dLLMs). Order robustness is defined as the ability to solve reasoning problems regardless of whether the reasoning chain precedes or follows the final answer. The authors compare three models: LLaDA-8B-Instruct (diffusion base), LLaDA-Instruct-JustGRPO (diffusion model fine-tuned with AR-order GRPO), and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (pure AR anchor). Evaluation spans two benchmarks (ReasonOrderQA and GSM8K) under CoT-First and Answer-First prompt conditions. Key findings show that JustGRPO significantly reduces order robustness, with robustness ratio drops of 0.192 on ReasonOrderQA and 0.138 on GSM8K, both exceeding the authors' decision threshold of 0.10. The degradation is concentrated at medium difficulty levels (D2–D3) requiring multi-step reasoning. While AR-order RL improves CoT-First accuracy by up to 19.6 percentage points, it simultaneously reduces Answer-First accuracy, revealing a fundamental accuracy-robustness trade-off. JustGRPO's robustness profile sits approximately 53% of the way between the diffusion base and the AR anchor. All experiments are fully deterministic (temperature=0.0), and longer generation contexts amplify the robustness degradation. Dataset licenses and independent stochastic runs are not specified.

# Abstract
- Diffusion language models (dLLMs) possess order robustness, unlike autoregressive models.
- The dominant post-training method, JustGRPO, uses AR-order (left-to-right) reward signals.
- The study evaluates whether AR-order RL training compromises order robustness.
- Models compared: LLaDA-8B-Instruct (base), LLaDA-Instruct-JustGRPO (AR-order RL), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (AR anchor).

# Introduction
- dLLMs generate text via iterative denoising, enabling parallel generation and flexible output orders.
- Order robustness allows solving reasoning problems regardless of output order, a property fundamentally absent in AR models.
- JustGRPO applies Group Relative Policy Optimization with left-to-right token-level rewards, raising concerns about compromising dLLM order flexibility.
- Experimental design isolates AR-order RL effects by comparing the diffusion base, the JustGRPO-fine-tuned diffusion model, and an AR anchor across two prompt conditions (CoT-First and Answer-First).
- Contributions explicitly stated:
  - JustGRPO reduces robustness ratio by 0.192 (ReasonOrderQA) and 0.138 (GSM8K), exceeding the 0.10 decision threshold.
  - JustGRPO's robustness profile covers ~53% of the gap to the AR anchor, indicating partial internalization of AR-style order dependence.
  - Degradation is concentrated at D2–D3 difficulty levels; simple problems (D1) remain robust.
  - Fundamental trade-off identified: CoT-First accuracy improves by up to 19.6 percentage points at the cost of reduced order flexibility.

# Experiments
- Problem Formulation: Robustness ratio defined as r = Acc(AF)/Acc(CF). r=1 indicates perfect robustness; r<1 indicates degradation. Decision threshold for significant reduction set at Δr ≥ 0.10.
- Models: LLaDA-8B-Instruct (8B parameters, diffusion base), LLaDA-Instruct-JustGRPO (8B parameters, AR-order RL post-training), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (7B parameters, AR anchor).
- Benchmarks: ReasonOrderQA (1,000 arithmetic reasoning problems, difficulty levels D1–D4) and GSM8K (1,319 grade-school math word problems).
- Generation Settings: Diffusion models use steps=256, gen_length=512, block_length=32, temperature=0.0, and low-confidence remasking. AR model uses greedy decoding (temperature=0.0) with max_new_tokens=512. All experiments are fully deterministic.
- Main Results:
  - ReasonOrderQA: Base r=0.698, JustGRPO r=0.506 (Δr=-0.192), Qwen r=0.339.
  - GSM8K: Base r=0.606, JustGRPO r=0.468 (Δr=-0.138), Qwen r=0.340.
  - CoT-First accuracy gains: +19.6 percentage points on ReasonOrderQA (68.9% to 88.5%), +15.0 percentage points on GSM8K (73.5% to 88.5%).
  - Answer-First accuracy slightly decreases: ReasonOrderQA drops from 48.1% to 44.8%; GSM8K drops from 44.5% to 41.4%.
  - JustGRPO covers 53.5% of the robustness gap to the AR anchor on ReasonOrderQA, calculated as (0.698 - 0.506)/(0.698 - 0.339).
- Per-Difficulty Analysis:
  - D1 (easy): High robustness maintained across models. Base r=0.963, JustGRPO r=0.917, Qwen r=0.844. Δr=-0.046.
  - D2–D3 (multi-step): Pronounced degradation. D2 drops from 0.635 to 0.407 (Δr=-0.228). D3 drops from 0.527 to 0.288 (Δr=-0.239).
  - D4 (hard): Floor effect observed; all models achieve r=0 due to zero Answer-First accuracy.
- Canvas Length Ablation:
  - Shorter canvas (L=64): Base r=0.846, JustGRPO r=0.701. Robustness gap=0.145.
  - Standard canvas (L=512): Base r=0.698, JustGRPO r=0.506. Robustness gap=0.192.
  - Gap ratio=0.756, indicating longer generation contexts amplify robustness degradation.
- Deterministic Verification: 4,000 total outputs (2 models × 2 conditions × 1,000 problems) verified bitwise identical across runs. Zero sampling variance. Dataset license and number of independent training runs not specified in this section.
