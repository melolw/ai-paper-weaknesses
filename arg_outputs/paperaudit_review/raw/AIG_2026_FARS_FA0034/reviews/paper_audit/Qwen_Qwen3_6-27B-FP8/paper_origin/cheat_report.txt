# Integrity and Consistency Risk Report

**Manuscript Title:** ADAPTIVE RERANK BUDGETING FOR VIDEO-TEXT RETRIEVAL VIA LAYER-DISAGREEMENT ROUTING
**Assessment Date:** Current
**Reviewer Role:** Expert Research Paper Reviewer

## Executive Summary
This manuscript presents a training-free method for adaptive reranking budget allocation. A critical observation is that the Abstract explicitly contains a warning: *"WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system."* Additionally, the reference list cites works from 2025 and 2026 (e.g., Tzachor et al., 2026; Skean et al., 2025), which is consistent with the synthetic nature of the document but indicates the paper is not a standard human-authored submission.

Beyond the explicit admission of automated generation, a **clear internal inconsistency** was identified between the textual claims in the Ablation Study (Section 4.3) and the data presented in Table 2.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Contradiction in Ablation Study Claims (Section 4.3 vs. Table 2)
**Severity:** High (Factual Mismatch)
**Location:** Section 4.3 (Text) vs. Table 2 (Data)

*   **Claim:** In Section 4.3, the authors state: *"The layer-disagreement signal is robust to design choices: alternative layer sets ({18, 24, 27}) and alternative metrics (Kendall τ) **all outperform or match the margin baseline**."*
*   **Evidence:** Table 2 presents the following Recall@1 (R@1) scores on MSR-VTT:
    *   **Margin baseline:** 52.3
    *   **Alt layers ({18, 24, 27}):** 52.0
*   **Inconsistency:** The "Alt layers" variant achieves an R@1 of 52.0, which is **lower** than the margin baseline of 52.3. Therefore, the claim that it "outperforms or matches" the baseline is factually incorrect based on the provided table. The text overstates the robustness of the method regarding layer selection.

### 2. Formatting Artifacts in Figure 1 Caption
**Severity:** Low (Presentation Error)
**Location:** Figure 1 Caption (Block #3)

*   **Issue:** The caption contains garbled checkbox characters: *"Easy queries (63%): K=10 [ ] Medium (32%): K=60 [x] Hard (5%): K=100"*.
*   **Context:** The image legend (Block #4) uses distinct icons (Green Check, Yellow Circle, Red X) to represent these tiers. The caption text appears to be a failed copy-paste or OCR artifact of these visual symbols. While not a scientific error, it indicates a lack of proofreading in the final manuscript generation.

### 3. Contextual Integrity Note: Automated Generation
**Severity:** Critical (Contextual)
**Location:** Abstract

*   **Observation:** The Abstract includes the sentence: *"WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system. The code is publicly available."*
*   **Implication:** In a standard academic context, submitting a paper with this disclaimer would constitute a major integrity violation unless the paper is specifically about automated research systems. The presence of future-dated references (2025, 2026) further confirms the synthetic origin. This report focuses on internal consistency, but this explicit admission is the primary integrity marker of the document.

## Conclusion
The manuscript contains a **clear factual contradiction** in Section 4.3 where the text claims an ablation variant outperforms the baseline, while Table 2 shows it underperforms. Additionally, the paper explicitly identifies itself as AI-generated, which fundamentally alters its standing as a human-authored scientific contribution. No other major numerical or logical inconsistencies were found between the Method, Results, and Tables.
