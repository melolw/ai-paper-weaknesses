The minor rounding difference between the text (164.86) and table (164.85) for $k=1$ position is negligible and does not affect conclusions.

## Logical & Methodological Assessment
- **Hypothesis Testing**: The paper clearly states its hypothesis (position bias is the primary bottleneck), proposes a targeted intervention (debiased one-pass sorting), and correctly interprets the results as a refutation of the hypothesis. The conclusion that iterative sorting provides benefits beyond bias correction (e.g., attention context refinement) logically follows from the residual performance gaps.
- **Methodological Transparency**: Section 3.3 clearly outlines the bias estimation pipeline (trimming top $\alpha=0.05$, binning into $B=20$, median interpolation). The distinction between additive and divisive debiasing is noted, though the specific variant used in experiments is not explicitly stated. This is a minor reporting gap but does not undermine the validity of the reported results.
- **Experimental Design**: The use of three random seeds (42, 123, 456) with 200 examples each (600 total) provides adequate statistical grounding for the claims. The paired comparison claim ("0 wins, 600 ties, 0 losses") is consistent with the identical mean accuracies reported.
