### Integrity and Consistency Risk Report

**Manuscript:** WINDOWSCAN-JUDGE: ROBUST SAFETY JUDGING AGAINST BENIGN-PADDING ATTACKS VIA WINDOWED SCANNING AND LENGTH-AWARE AGGREGATION

#### 1. Inconsistent Definition and Application of FPR Budget
**Severity:** High  
**Location:** Section 4.1 (Experimental Setup), Section 4.3 (Main Results), Table 1.

**Issue:** There is a clear logical inconsistency between the definition of the False Positive Rate (FPR) budget in the methodology and its application in the results analysis. This inconsistency materially affects the interpretation of the method's effectiveness.

*   **Definition (Section 4.1):** The manuscript states, "The FPR budget is set to $\delta = \text{FPR}_{\text{holistic}} + 0.05$." The phrasing "relative to the holistic baseline" strongly implies a **per-condition budget**, where the allowable FPR for each padding condition (Original, Append Long, Prepend+Append, Interleaved) is calculated based on the holistic judge's FPR on that specific condition.
*   **Application (Section 4.3 & Table 1):** The results analysis implies a **global budget** derived solely from the "Original" condition, applied uniformly across all padding conditions.
    *   For WildGuard, the text claims the budget is $\delta = 0.1868$. This matches the Original condition's holistic FPR ($0.137$) plus $0.05$.
    *   For Llama Guard 3, the text claims the budget is $0.1974$. This matches the Original condition's holistic FPR ($0.147$) plus $0.05$.
*   **Impact:** Under a per-condition interpretation (as suggested by the definition), the method fails to control FPR on padded conditions where the baseline has perfect specificity.
    *   **WildGuard (Table 1):** On "Append Long" and "Prepend+Append", the holistic baseline FPR is $0.000$. The WSJ method yields FPRs of $0.147$ and $0.179$, respectively. These represent massive increases in false positives, yet the text claims FPR is "controlled within budget" because these values are below the global budget of $0.1868$ (derived from the Original condition).
    *   This creates a misleading narrative: the method is presented as maintaining FPR within budget, while in reality, it degrades specificity from $0\%$ to $\sim15\text{--}18\%$ on conditions where the baseline was perfect. The definition in Section 4.1 does not support the global application used in Section 4.3.

#### 2. Minor Numerical Rounding Discrepancies
**Severity:** Low  
**Location:** Abstract, Section 4.2, Section 4.3 vs. Table 1.

**Issue:** There are minor inconsistencies in numerical precision between the text/abstract and the tables, likely due to rounding. While not indicative of misconduct, they reduce precision.
*   **Abstract:** Claims WildGuard's FNR increases from $0.0455$ to $1.0$. **Table 1** lists Original FNR as $.045$ and Prepend+Append FNR as $1.00$.
*   **Abstract:** Claims WSJ reduces FNR to $0.0091$. **Table 1** lists WSJ-WildGuard Prepend+Append FNR as $.009$.
*   **Section 4.3:** Claims F1 improves to $0.9237$. **Table 1** lists F1 as $.924$.
*   **Section 4.2:** Claims Llama Guard 3 FNR increases from $0.0273$ to $0.2636$. **Table 1** lists $.027$ and $.264$.

#### 3. Disclosure of Synthetic Origin
**Severity:** Informational  
**Location:** Abstract (Warning Note), References.

**Issue:** The manuscript explicitly states: "WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system." Additionally, the reference list contains multiple citations with future dates (e.g., Zhao et al., 2025; Maloyan et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025a/b), and arXiv IDs corresponding to late 2025 (e.g., `2510.14276`).
*   **Assessment:** This is a disclosure rather than an integrity violation, but it contextualizes the potential for hallucinated metrics or references. The internal consistency of the FPR budget issue (Point 1) suggests the automated system may have generated results and text that are logically misaligned regarding metric definitions.

### Conclusion
The primary integrity risk is the **misalignment between the FPR budget definition and its application**. The manuscript defines a budget relative to the holistic baseline but applies a global budget derived from the "Original" condition to justify FPR control on padded conditions where the baseline FPR is zero. This masks significant degradation in specificity (FPR increase from $0.0$ to $\sim0.18$) and renders the claim of "maintaining FPR within budget" misleading under the stated definition.
