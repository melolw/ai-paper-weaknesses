# Integrity Risk and Consistency Report

**Manuscript Title:** ENTITY-ANONYMIZED CONTEXT PROMPTS FOR IMPROVING CONTEXT FAITHFULNESS IN KNOWLEDGE-CONFLICT QA
**Assessment Date:** Current
**Reviewer Focus:** Internal inconsistencies, reference integrity, logical contradictions.

## Executive Summary
This manuscript discloses that it was generated by an automated research system. While the experimental design is clearly structured, there are **critical integrity risks** related to reference fabrication and citation inconsistencies. Specifically, multiple references are dated in the future (2025, 2026), and there is a direct contradiction between the text and reference list regarding the publication year of a key related work. These issues severely undermine the trustworthiness of the literature review and the validity of the comparative claims.

## 1. Reference Integrity and Fabrication Risks
**Severity: High**

*   **Future-Dated References:** The reference list contains multiple entries with publication years in the future relative to the current date (2024/2025). This is a strong indicator of hallucination or fabrication of citations.
    *   **Anand et al.:** Cited as `2026` in both the text (Section 2, Section 4.3) and the reference list (`abs/2601.04131, 2026`). An ArXiv ID starting with `2601` implies January 2026.
    *   **Huang et al.:** Cited as `2025` in both the text (Section 2) and the reference list (`2025`).
    *   **Impact:** These future dates suggest the references do not exist or are hallucinated, invalidating the related work discussion and the comparison with methods like "ContextFocus" and "ParamMute."

*   **Citation Year Mismatch (Sheikhi et al.):**
    *   **Text Claim:** Section 2 (Related Work) states: "Sheikhi et al. (**2025**) recently explored entity anonymization..."
    *   **Reference List:** The corresponding entry is: "Hadi Sheikhi... **2023**."
    *   **Impact:** This is a clear internal inconsistency. The text cites a future year (2025) while the reference list provides a past year (2023). This further supports the hypothesis of automated hallucination in the citation generation process.

## 2. Experimental Consistency and Validity
**Severity: Medium**

*   **Cross-Model Comparison with Context-DPO:**
    *   **Claim:** Section 4.2 states EACP "outperforms Context-DPO... (Pc=74.75% vs 54.9%)."
    *   **Inconsistency:** The authors acknowledge: "While these results are on different models (Llama-3.1-8B vs Qwen2-7B)..."
    *   **Issue:** The manuscript evaluates EACP on **Llama-3.1-8B** (Table 1) but compares it to Context-DPO results obtained on **Qwen2-7B** (as stated in the text). While the authors note this limitation, comparing performance across different model families and sizes without a controlled baseline on the *same* model weakens the claim of superiority. The reference to "Qwen2-7B" also conflicts slightly with the manuscript's own evaluation on "Qwen2.5-7B" (Section 4.4), suggesting potential confusion in model versioning.

*   **Subset Variance in Table 2:**
    *   **Observation:** Table 1 reports results on the full 6,000-example ConFiQA-MC benchmark. Table 2 reports results on a "1,500-example subset."
    *   **Inconsistency:** Condition C (EACP) achieves **Pc=74.75%** in Table 1 (full set) but **Pc=72.87%** in Table 2 (subset).
    *   **Impact:** While variance is expected, the drop in performance on the subset should be explicitly discussed to ensure the results in Table 2 (used for the ContextFocus comparison) are representative. The manuscript does not address this discrepancy.

## 3. Logical and Structural Observations
**Severity: Low**

*   **Automated Generation Disclosure:** The abstract includes a warning: "WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system." This disclosure is appropriate but contextualizes the errors found above (future dates, citation mismatches) as likely artifacts of the generation process rather than intentional misconduct. However, it does not mitigate the scientific validity issues.

## Conclusion
The manuscript contains **clear evidence of reference hallucination** (future dates) and **internal citation inconsistencies** (Sheikhi et al. year mismatch). These issues cast significant doubt on the accuracy of the related work and the validity of comparisons with methods like ContextFocus and ParamMute. The cross-model comparison with Context-DPO is also a validity concern. While the experimental design for EACP is logically sound, the integrity of the literature review and comparative analysis is compromised.

**Recommendation:** The authors must verify all references, correct the citation year for Sheikhi et al., and address the future-dated references. The comparison with Context-DPO should ideally be replicated on the same model family to ensure a fair comparison.
