# Academic Integrity & Consistency Risk Report

## Executive Summary
The manuscript presents a coherent diagnostic framework and claims that the "repetition advantage" in long-CoT SFT is primarily a termination effect. The internal logic is largely consistent, and the proposed mediation analysis aligns with the reported results. However, **two high-impact integrity/transparency flags** are present: an explicit disclosure of automated generation and multiple future-dated references, which significantly impact empirical trustworthiness. Additionally, minor numerical and methodological inconsistencies were identified.

## Key Findings

### 1. Automated Generation Disclosure (High Impact)
- **Evidence:** Abstract explicitly states: `*WARNING: This paper was generated by an automated research system. The code is publicly available.*`
- **Assessment:** While transparent, this disclosure fundamentally alters the evaluation of empirical validity. Results, tables, and statistical claims may be simulated or hallucinated by the generation system rather than derived from actual computational experiments. The provided code link mitigates traditional fabrication concerns but shifts the burden of verification to external reproduction.

### 2. Future-Dated References & Citation Anomalies (High Impact)
- **Evidence:** Multiple references are dated in the future relative to standard publication timelines:
  - `Kopiczko et al. (2026)` (Section 1, 2, 3.1; References)
  - `DeepSeek-AI et al. (2025)`, `Ettinger et al. (2025)`, `Kim et al. (2025)`, `Yang et al. (2025)`, `Ye et al. (2025)` (References)
- **Assessment:** Future-dated citations are highly atypical for peer-reviewed or archival work and strongly suggest AI-generated content with projected or hallucinated metadata. This raises concerns about the grounding of the related work and the actual existence/accessibility of cited datasets (e.g., `DOLCI-think`, `OLMo-3-7B` as of the reference date).

### 3. Numerical Mismatch in GPQA Gap Calculation (Medium Impact)
- **Evidence:** 
  - **Table 1** reports GPQA Acc@n for Condition A = 17.2% and Condition B = 37.6%. The difference is `37.6 - 17.2 = 20.4pp`.
  - **Figure 2 Caption** states the unconditional gap for GPQA is `+20.4pp`.
  - **Table 2** reports the GPQA Unconditional Gap as `+20.5pp`.
- **Assessment:** A 0.1pp discrepancy across tables/figures indicates minor calculation or rounding inconsistency. While not fatal, it suggests automated table generation or post-hoc editing without full numerical reconciliation.

### 4. Methodological Opacity in Ablation Hyperparameters (Low-Medium Impact)
- **Evidence:** 
  - **Table 3** lists varying $\alpha$ values for ablation variants: C ($\alpha=10.0$), C-EOS-only ($\alpha=50.0$), C-think-only ($\alpha=49.7$), C-no-cap ($\alpha=37.3$).
  - **Section 4.5** states: `removing the alpha cap (C-no-cap with α = 37.3) performs at baseline level... confirming that the moderate α = 10 used in the full method is preferable to the formula-computed values (37–50).`
- **Assessment:** The manuscript does not explain why single-token ablation variants use $\alpha \approx 50$ while the no-cap variant uses $\alpha=37.3$. The claim that these are "formula-computed values" lacks methodological detail, making it difficult to verify whether the ablation fairly isolates the effect of token type vs. hyperparameter tuning.

## Conclusion
The manuscript is internally logically consistent in its argument that termination behavior drives the repetition advantage. However, the **explicit automated generation warning** and **future-dated references** constitute major integrity/transparency flags that severely limit confidence in the empirical results without independent code verification. Minor numerical mismatches and unexplained ablation hyperparameters further suggest automated drafting artifacts. 

**Recommendation:** Treat empirical claims as provisional. Require independent reproduction of the provided code to validate tables, mediation fractions, and benchmark scores before considering the findings scientifically established.
