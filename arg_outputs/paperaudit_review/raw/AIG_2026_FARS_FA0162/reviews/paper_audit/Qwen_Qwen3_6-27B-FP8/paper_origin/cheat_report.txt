**Integrity Risk and Consistency Report**

**1. Critical Internal Inconsistency: Random Baseline Contradiction**
There is a major contradiction regarding the "Random" baseline performance between Table 1 and Figure 2, as well as a mathematical impossibility in Figure 2.
*   **Evidence:**
    *   **Table 1 (Section 4.2):** Reports Random Recall@32 as **0.030** (3.0%). This is consistent with uniform random selection over 1024 buckets ($32/1024 \approx 0.031$).
    *   **Figure 2 Table (Section 4.4):** Reports Random Recall@32 as **0.25** (25.0%). This contradicts Table 1 by a factor of ~8.
    *   **Mathematical Impossibility:** In the Figure 2 table, Random Recall@128 is reported as **0.88** (88%). For uniform random selection of 128 buckets out of 1024, the expected recall is $128/1024 = 0.125$ (12.5%). A recall of 88% implies that a random selection of 12.5% of buckets captures 88% of the attention mass, which is statistically impossible for a uniform random baseline unless the attention distribution is extremely pathological (and even then, the expectation would not be 0.88).
    *   **Impact:** This suggests the data in Figure 2 may be fabricated, hallucinated, or mislabeled (e.g., the "Random" row in Figure 2 might erroneously contain data from another method or a different experiment).

**2. Numerical Mismatch: Gap Closure Calculation in Figure 2**

*   **Evidence:**

This indicates a lack of internal consistency in the reported numbers.
